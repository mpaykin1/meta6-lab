About ABYSS GLOW

Every night someone dreams they are a small glowing fish. Swim into an endless dark sea that starts as a cute fairy reef and slowly turns into a nightmare: whispering kelp, sunken ruins, a forest of watching eyes and the abyss. Collect pearls to make your light stronger, dodge giant jaws that strike from the screen edges and from the depths, and reach the bottom of the dream to wake up. Pixel rim-lit art with 3D creatures and calm, shifting ambient music.

How to play ABYSS GLOW

Touch and hold the screen or move the mouse: the fish swims toward your finger or cursor. WASD or arrow keys also work. Collect glowing pearls: every 10 pearls restores a heart, 10 and 25 pearls give extra hearts and a stronger light. Avoid the creepy creatures and the jaws attacking from the edges and from below. Swim deeper through five zones until the abyss looks at you and you wake up. P pauses the game, the speaker button mutes sound.

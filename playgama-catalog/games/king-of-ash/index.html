About King of Ash

A voxel arena brawler where a crowned king with a sword fights endless waves of skeletons inside a burning colosseum. Chain acrobatic sword moves with simple gestures: dash thrusts, corkscrew leaps, flips, cartwheels and tornado spins. Fill your rage bar to unleash royal ultimates like Wrath of Heaven and Earthsplitter. Pixel-art 2D effects layered over a moody 3D voxel world with fire, lightning and ash.

How to play King of Ash

Survive the skeleton waves and smash as many as you can. Tap to dash-thrust, double tap to roll (invulnerable), swipe sideways for a corkscrew leap, swipe up to launch enemies, swipe down for a shockwave, swipe diagonally for spin kicks, draw a circle for a tornado, hold to block and counter. With full rage use two-finger swipes for ultimate moves. On desktop, use the mouse: click and drag to swipe. The hand button shows the full move list.

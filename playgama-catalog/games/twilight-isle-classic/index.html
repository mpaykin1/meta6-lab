About Twilight Isle Classic

A voxel island action game at dusk. Wash ashore empty-handed, collect every coin, arm yourself with weapons scattered around the island and defeat the Lighthouse Warden before the shadows take over. Choose your hero, find over 20 weapons from swords and bows to plasma guns, pick up relics with passive powers and light the lighthouse before night wins.

How to play Twilight Isle Classic

Collect all coins on the island, find weapons, survive the night and defeat the Lighthouse Warden to light the lighthouse. Desktop: WASD to move, Space to jump, left mouse or J to attack, Q/E or 1-0 to switch weapons, R to reload, right mouse to rotate the camera, Esc for the arsenal. Mobile: left thumb moves, right thumb turns the camera, on-screen buttons attack, jump and switch weapons.
